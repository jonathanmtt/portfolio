FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need, whether you want a second language and which features make sense for your business. After your free preview, I send you one fixed quote — no hourly billing, no surprises. You only pay the deposit if you decide to go ahead.

What's included?

Every site includes a mobile-friendly design, tap-to-call, a quote request form, your reviews and map, SSL security and basic SEO, plus two rounds of revisions. Extra pages and a second language are quoted separately.

How long does it take?

Most standard sites are ready in 5–7 business days after you approve the scope, pay the deposit and send your content: logo, photos and the services you offer.

Do I own my website?

Your domain is registered in your name, and the finished site files are yours once the project is paid in full. If you ever want to move on, I'll hand everything over.

What happens after launch?

Your site is live and it's yours. If you'd like, an optional monthly care plan covers hosting, backups, fixes and small updates — or you can take the keys and manage it yourself.
