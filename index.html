FAQ

Questions owners actually ask me

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need, whether you want it in two languages and which features make sense for your business. After your free mockup, I send you one fixed price — no hourly billing, no surprises. You only pay the deposit if you decide to go ahead.

Is it really custom, or a template?

Every site is designed around your business: your services, your area, your reviews, your colors. I reuse what's proven to work — fast, clear layouts — so you don't pay for reinventing the wheel.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and the site files are yours after the final payment. If you ever want to leave, I'll hand everything over.

What do you need from me?

Your logo (if you have one), a few photos of your work, your services and the areas you serve. I write the text — you just review it.

Can you guarantee I'll be #1 on Google?

No — and be careful with anyone who does. I set up the basics right (speed, mobile, Google Business Profile, local SEO) so customers can find you and trust what they see.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace?

You can, if you have the time. Most owners I talk to would rather spend their weekends on their business. With me you get a custom site, done for you, without paying a builder every month.

What happens after launch?

Your site is live and yours. Add an optional monthly care plan and I handle hosting, updates and changes — or take the keys and manage it yourself.

Do you do Spanish or Portuguese sites?

Yes. I work in English and Portuguese myself. For Spanish versions, I translate carefully and recommend a quick review by a native speaker on your team before launch.

Do you only work locally?

No. I work with businesses anywhere in the US by text, phone and video. If you're nearby, I'm happy to meet in person.
