FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything homeowners ask before their first clean. Don't see yours? We're happy to talk it through.

Talk to a real personCall or text (619) 555-0142, Mon–Sat.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Nope, most of our clients aren't. You can leave a key, share a lockbox code or meet us for the first visit. We'll text you when we arrive and again when we lock up.

What's the difference between a standard and a deep clean?

A standard clean keeps your home in great shape: kitchen, bathrooms, dusting, floors and tidying. A deep clean adds the detail work, like baseboards, door frames, light switches, cabinet fronts, inside the microwave and built-up grime. Most new clients start with a deep clean, then switch to recurring visits.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Every team arrives with eco-friendly, pet- and kid-safe products, fresh microfiber and HEPA-filter vacuums. If you'd like us to use a specific product of yours, just let us know.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll send the team back to re-clean it, free. No forms, no runaround. It's a big part of why 96% of our clients rebook.

How does pricing work? Is there a contract?

You get a flat price upfront based on your home's size and the service, never an open-ended hourly rate. Recurring plans save up to 20%, and there are no contracts: skip, pause or cancel with 48 hours' notice.
