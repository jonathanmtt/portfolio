FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Don't see yours? Call (760) 555-0126 and you'll talk to an owner, not a call center.

Can I get a rebate for replacing my lawn?

Possibly. Many North County homeowners may qualify for local water-district rebates for removing turf and upgrading to efficient irrigation. Programs and requirements change, so we'll help you check what's available for your address and design the project to meet the guidelines.

Some programs require approval before any lawn comes out, so talk to us before you start digging.

Will a drought-tolerant yard just look like rocks and cactus?

Not the way we do it. We layer native and Mediterranean plants (sages, grasses, succulents and flowering shrubs) for color and texture all year, with mulch or decomposed granite between beds. It looks lush; it just doesn't need daily watering.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Fernway is a licensed California landscape contractor (CA Lic. #000000 (demo)) and carries general liability and workers' comp insurance. We're happy to send certificates before work starts.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. We offer weekly, every-other-week and monthly plans covering mowing, pruning, weeding, irrigation checks and seasonal cleanups. Many clients start with an install and keep us on to care for it.

How soon can you start?

Estimates are usually scheduled within a few days. Install timing depends on the season and project size, and you'll get a real start date along with your quote. Fall and winter are ideal for planting natives, so those spots fill up first.
