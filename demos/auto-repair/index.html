05 FAQ

Questions we hear every day.

Don't see yours? Call (702) 555-0178. A technician picks up, not a call center.

Do you work on my make?

Almost certainly. We service domestic, Japanese, Korean and European cars, trucks and SUVs, including hybrids. If a job ever needs dealer-only equipment, we'll tell you up front instead of guessing.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments get priority, but we take walk-ins for oil changes, check-engine scans and A/C checks whenever a bay is open. Call ahead and we'll give you an honest wait time.

How long does a brake job take?

Most pad and rotor replacements take about two hours. Drop off in the morning and you'll usually have your car back by lunch. We text you the moment it's ready.

What does the 24/24 warranty cover?

Parts and labor on covered repairs for 24 months or 24,000 miles, whichever comes first. It's printed on every invoice, so there's never a question about what's included.

Will servicing here void my factory warranty?

No. Federal law lets you have your car maintained at any qualified shop. We use parts that meet factory specs and keep a detailed record of every service for your files.
