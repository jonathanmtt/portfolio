Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This website is run by Jonathan Mattos, an independent web developer based in the United States. This policy explains what information I collect through this site and what I do with it. In short: I only use it to reply to you and to do the work you ask for. I never sell it.

What I collect

  • What you send me: when you use the contact form, I receive your name, business name, email, and — if you choose to share them — your phone number, website and message.
  • Basic, anonymous visit statistics (if enabled): pages viewed, approximate country and device type. This does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

How I use it

  • To reply to your request and send you the free mockup you asked for.
  • To plan, build and support your website if we work together.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve this site.

Who else handles it

I use a small number of trusted services to run this site: a hosting provider (Cloudflare) and a form-delivery service (Web3Forms) that forwards your message to my email. They process data only to provide those services. I don't sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't share it for advertising.

How long I keep it

I keep your messages for as long as needed to answer you and, if we work together, for the length of the project and my business records. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the information you sent me — just email me (address below) and I'll take care of it within 30 days.

Do Not Track

This site doesn't track you across other websites and doesn't use advertising cookies, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Children

This site is meant for business owners. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
